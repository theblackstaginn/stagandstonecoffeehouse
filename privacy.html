Privacy & Email Updates

Last updated October 9, 2026.

When you join Stag & Stone Coffee and Bakehouse's mailing list, we record your email address, signup date, source and consent to receive our updates. Signup is optional.

What you'll receive

Opening announcements, seasonal menu news, and occasional community and event updates from Stag & Stone in Clarkesville, Georgia.

How we handle your information

Your email address is kept in our access-controlled business subscriber database. We do not sell subscriber addresses. When an email delivery service is connected, it may process subscriber addresses on our behalf to send the updates you requested.

Unsubscribe and deletion

You can stop receiving emails or ask us to delete your signup by emailing stagandstonecoffee@gmail.com. Once sending is enabled, messages will include unsubscribe instructions.

Questions

Contact stagandstonecoffee@gmail.com with questions about your information.
